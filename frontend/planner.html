Data sources and simulation assumptions

Task names are adapted from NASA mission reports and reference procedures. Schedule dates, durations, fictional profiles, readings and stress coefficients are demo assumptions, not historical astronaut measurements. EveryWear is an application connected to biomedical sensors, not itself a sensor.

Calendar DCS estimates use the existing model.js forward model. Consecutive oxygen stages are converted to equivalent washout inputs. Hydration and general stress have no validated numeric DCS coefficient here. Prior-EVA carryover uses the existing model's assumed equal spacing based on the latest gap. EVA duration changes oxygen exposure but not DCS in this model. Predicted monitor readings do not diagnose DCS or measure DLCO. Pressure containment failure probability is not modeled.

No automated medical clearance. Mission-approved protocols and medical review govern operational use.
